WHY WE'RE RIDING

Built in the wilderness.
Riding for its future.

We’re Bam Zell and Nelson Geraghty. Long before this ride, canoe trips through Ontario taught us what it means to move through wild places under our own power—and why those places are worth protecting.

In 2027, we plan to leave Alaska by bicycle and ride south through the Americas toward Argentina. 2WHEELS1PLANET is our way of turning an expedition into something bigger: a platform for conservation, storytelling, and the landscapes that made us who we are.

Our final route, conservation fundraiser, and expedition tracking will be added as planning is completed.

THE EXPEDITION

North to south.
Human powered.

Beginning in Anchorage, Alaska, the route will cross Canada, the United States, Mexico and Central America before continuing through South America toward Tierra del Fuego. The Darién Gap will be bypassed by legal transport rather than crossed overland.

Bam Zell
