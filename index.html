THE CHALLENGE

One continent.
Two wheels.
A reason to ride.

We’re taking on an enormous human-powered journey from Alaska to Argentina, crossing the Americas by bicycle.

The goal isn’t simply to reach the other end. We want to use the expedition to tell stories about wild places, meet people working to protect them, and turn miles ridden into support for conservation.

THE MISSION

Every mile has a purpose.

Across the route are forests, mountains, rivers, deserts, coastlines and communities whose futures are tied to healthy ecosystems.

We’re partnering with a conservation nonprofit to turn the attention generated by the expedition into real support for protecting wild places and wildlife.
